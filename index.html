Hangxing Zhang

I am a senior undergraduate student in Computer Science at ShanghaiTech University. I am currently a research intern at MIT CSAIL with Prof. Wojciech Matusik, and at Harvard SEAS with Prof. Heng Yang and Prof. Yilun Du. During the 2025–26 academic year, I was a visiting undergraduate student at Harvard University and a cross-registered student at MIT.

My goal is to build robots that perceive and act in the physical world as skillfully as humans do, by tightly coupling touch, vision, and learning. My current interests include:

  • Dexterous manipulation — contact-rich, multi-fingered manipulation with real robot hands.
  • Tactile sensing — using high-resolution touch to perceive contact and guide action.
  • 3D vision for robotics — geometry-grounded perception, from calibration to scene understanding.
  • Robot learning — scalable policies learned from human and robot demonstrations.

I am applying for PhD programs starting Fall 2027.
